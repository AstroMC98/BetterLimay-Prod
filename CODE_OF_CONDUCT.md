# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

BetterLimay contributors are expected to make participation welcoming, respectful, factual, and safe. We welcome people across backgrounds and experience levels, and we discuss public-interest technology without harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior includes publishing private personal information, impersonating the LGU or another contributor, manipulating civic records, abusing issue/report channels, sexualized or discriminatory conduct, and repeated disruptive behavior after a moderator request.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, reject, or remove contributions that violate this code, and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the public record. Data corrections are welcome; harassment is not a correction method.

## Reporting

Report safety or conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the repository's configured private contact channel. **Maintainer contact: TODO — configure and publish a monitored contact before launch.** Do not include report contents, passwords, access tokens, or unnecessary personal data in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith, with confidentiality handled as far as practical. If there is an immediate threat to safety, contact the appropriate emergency service rather than relying on this repository.
