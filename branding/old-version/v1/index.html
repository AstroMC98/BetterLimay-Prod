BetterLimay

An initiative of BetterGov.PH for Limay, Bataan

This guide covers the BetterLimay emblem, what each part means, and how to use it across posts, print and BetterLimay.org.

Design story

The emblem answers one question: what does Limay look like to the people who live here? It is built from four ideas, each carrying one meaning, in the same format BetterGov.PH uses to explain its own mark.

How we got here

  1. Start from the place. People describe Limay by its mountains (Mount Limay and the Mariveles range), by the Petron Bataan Refinery in Lamao, the country's only remaining oil refinery, and by its role as an energy hub for Luzon.
  2. Make the land spell the name. The snowfield on the main peak is cut into an L, so the mountain carries the town's initial without needing a letterform on top.
  3. Join land and industry. Instead of standing beside the mountain, the refinery is set into its slope, turning two symbols into one silhouette: development that grows from the land, not on top of it.
  4. Raise the flag sun. The eight-rayed sun ties the chapter to the nation and to BetterGov.PH, whose mark uses the same sun. It is the one shared element between parent and chapter.
  5. Keep it one colour. A single blue (#0A3496), close to the flag blue of BetterGov.PH, so the emblem survives photocopies, tarpaulins, embroidery and one-colour screen prints.

On the refinery. Industry is Limay's livelihood and also a source of real community concern about air, ash and the coastline. Present it as work, energy and responsibility, never as corporate promotion. BetterLimay's job is clear information about it, not endorsement.

Logo

BetterLimay is an endorsed brand: it has its own emblem and always carries the line “An initiative of BetterGov.PH”. Use the chapter lockup on everything the organization publishes. Use the place lockup only when the town itself is the subject, such as fiesta or town-pride material.

The wordmark is Montserrat: “Better” in Medium, “Limay” in ExtraBold. The emblem sits at roughly the height of both text lines, with a gap of about a sixth of its width.

BetterLimay is a citizen initiative, not the municipal government. Never place the emblem beside or in place of the official Municipality of Limay seal. Page bios should say: “Hindi opisyal na account ng Pamahalaang Bayan ng Limay.”

Logo usage

Clear space

Keep a quarter of the emblem's width free on every side.

Don't stretch

Scale proportionally only.

Don't recolour

Blue or white only. No gradients.

Don't rotate

The sun always rises straight up.

No busy grounds

On photos, place it on a solid blue tile.

Colour

Blue leads. A typical layout is mostly white or paper, about a third blue, and a small amount of accent. Gold highlights and red only warns.

Voice

Plain, local and useful. Write like a neighbour who works at the munisipyo and actually answers the question. Use Filipino first for community posts and English for partners and formal documents. Taglish is fine when that is how the audience talks.

Lead with what the reader can do: who qualifies, where to go, and until when. Every post names its source office and date, because transparency is the promise.

Write like this

“Bukas na ang aplikasyon para sa scholarship ng bayan hanggang Oktubre 15. Dalhin ang report card at barangay clearance sa Municipal Scholarship Office.”

Not like this

“GRABE!!! May scholarship na!!! I-share na ninyo bago mawala!!!”

Avoid hype, political colours, candidate names, blame and unverified claims.

Templates

The standard announcement post. Program and news posts use the gold bar; urgent advisories use red. Everything else stays the same. The examples are illustrative, not real announcements. Export at 1080 × 1080 for feeds and 1080 × 1920 for stories.

Rollout

Before launch

  • Get written approval from BetterGov.PH for the endorsement line and the shared sun element.
  • Have a designer clean up the vector emblem. This one was traced from the concept artwork and needs final node cleanup before large-format print.
  • Export the master set: chapter, place and stacked lockups in blue, white and black.

Digital launch

  • Facebook profile picture (emblem on a blue tile), cover image and a bio with the not-an-LGU-account line.
  • BetterLimay.org header, favicon and share image.
  • Pin a launch post explaining the four symbols using the design story panels.

Templates and community

  • Announcement and advisory templates in Canva for volunteers.
  • Tarpaulins, help-desk signage, volunteer shirts and lanyards in one-colour print.
  • Presence at the Pagbubunyi Festival (May 4) and the Apo Iko fiesta (October 4).
